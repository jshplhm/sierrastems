A florist collective.

Florists order together and share the shipping. Each trip to the growers is split evenly between the florists who order on it, so the more of us order, the less each of us pays.

  • Local when in season

    Stems from our Nevada County farm when they are in season. Central Coast growers in Monterey County the rest of the year. Imported roses available as needed.

  • Every stem has a source

    Each listing names the county it came from, like Monterey County, Santa Cruz County, or Sonoma County, so you can tell your clients exactly where their flowers grew.

  • A fresh list every week

    What we have changes with the season, so the list changes every week. Order straight from it.
