Substitutions

Flowers are a crop, and weather changes what we can cut. If a confirmed flower falls short on quality or count, we tell you before pickup or delivery and offer the closest match in color, form, and value. We do not substitute focal flowers without your OK. If you want no substitutions at all, say so in your order notes and we will credit anything we cannot fill.

Quality

Check your flowers at pickup or delivery. If something is not right, send a photo the same day and we will make it right.
