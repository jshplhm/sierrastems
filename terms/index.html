Substitutions

Flowers are a crop, and weather changes what we can cut. If a confirmed flower falls short on quality or count, we tell you before pickup or delivery and offer the closest match in color, form, and value. If you want no substitutions at all, say so in your order notes and we will credit anything we cannot fill.
