Sourcing

Every source named.

In season, the florist list comes from our own farm in Nevada County. Off season, we buy direct from California growers on the Central Coast. Imported roses are available as needed. The list names the source for every item.

The wholesale list is sourced separately and also includes some imported flowers, such as Ecuadorian roses. Its item names say so.

Off season

Partner growers, Central Coast

When our fields are resting, we source direct from growers in Santa Cruz, Sonoma, Yolo, and Monterey County.

When a design calls for roses we cannot get locally, we bring in imported roses as needed and list them as imported. Buying direct means we know who grew it, when it was cut, and how it traveled. Off-season orders carry a {% include money.html n=site.terms.offseason_minimum %} minimum, sized for larger weddings.
